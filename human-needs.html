The map

Five layers of human need

Needs stack like a pyramid (Maslow). The bottom ones must be met every day; the top ones appear once the bottom is safe. Businesses exist at every layer — but the bottom layers are the biggest markets, and the top layers carry the highest margins. Tap a layer to filter.

How an American household spends $78,535 a year

Needs become markets when people pay for them. Half of everything goes to just two needs — a home and getting around.
