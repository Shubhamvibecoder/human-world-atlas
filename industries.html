Every industry on Earth,
measured in America.

The USA publishes the cleanest numbers in the world on its economy. Understand how its $30.9 trillion economy is split — by industry, by jobs, and by company size — and you understand how every economy turns human needs into businesses. Follow the thread from the earth to the government.

Small, medium, large, very large

6.4 million companies — and 2,262 giants

Nearly 9 in 10 employer firms have fewer than 20 people. Yet the 2,262 companies with 5,000+ employees (0.04% of firms) employ over a third of all workers and take nearly half of all sales. Both worlds are huge — they're just different games.

Source: Census Statistics of US Businesses 2022 (released April 2025) — employer firms, employment in March 2022, annual receipts. Excludes farms, government, private households and railroads. "One-person businesses" from Census Nonemployer Statistics 2022. The SBA counts 36.2M small businesses in all (incl. one-person) and says they hold 45.9% of private jobs.
